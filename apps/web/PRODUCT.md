## Users

- **Hackathon judges (primary audience for public-facing pages).** They evaluate TAPat at the Velo x Noir Agentic Payment Hackathon and need to grasp, fast, which money decisions the software makes and what enforces them. Confirmed by the team, Nov 7 2026.
- **LGU program officer.** Creates the program, uploads the masterlist, reviews uncertain records, freezes stations, ends the program.
- **TODA enrollment officer.** Verifies a driver and taps their card on a phone.
- **Station attendant.** Enters the peso amount and has the driver tap.
- **Tricycle driver.** Taps an NFC card to pay for fuel and gets a Telegram receipt. Has no app screen.
- **COA auditor.** Exports a per-event CSV with a tx hash behind every number.
- **LGU Budget Office.** Pays the program budget through a Velo checkout.

## Product Purpose

TAPat lets an AI agent release, cap, and return fuel aid for tricycle drivers. It answers the trade-off every aid program faces: safe and slow, or fast and leaky. Success is a closed loop: a clean list in, a driver enrolled in a minute, and an agent that funds, refuses, and returns money.

## Positioning

Three money decisions made by software, each inside limits the LGU signs and enforced on-chain:

- **Fund:** the Tranche Agent releases a ₱1,500 escrow per card only when a DOE diesel price shift is real and inside a Velo-verified budget.
- **Refuse:** a per-card Noir policy caps, expires, and nonces every tap; the Freeze Agent pauses anomalous stations.
- **Return:** at program end, unspent escrow is swept back to the LGU wallet the same day.

## Operating Context

- Drivers are served by a card or sticker plus Telegram; they never need an app or mobile data.
- Prototype on Stellar Testnet. Submission Nov 7 2026, face-to-face pitch Nov 8 2026.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Program defaults: ₱500 per-tap cap, ₱1,500 tranche per card, ₱10,000 budget deposit, ₱65.00/L price threshold, demo rate ₱10 per XLM.
- Noir is called only through `@noirx402/agent-sdk`. Velo verifies the budget checkout and sponsors every Noir call; Velo does not verify per-tap payments, and no surface may claim it does.
- Card identity is privacy-scoped: show `displayName` ("Juan D.") and `…` plus the last 4 of a UID, never a full name or full hash.
- Basic auth covers the whole deployed site (Caddy; optionally `middleware.ts`), so a public page behind the Funnel URL still asks for the password. Changing that needs Jim.

## Product Principles

- Show the rule, not the promise: every claim points at the limit or contract that enforces it.
- Refusal is a feature: a declined tap is the product working, and it writes no chain transaction.
- Simulated stays labelled: simulated or precomputed data is marked as such everywhere.
- Every number traces to a real row and a tx hash.
- The driver carries nothing new: a card, no app, no data plan.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Status is always written out, so colour is never the only signal. Body text meets 4.5:1 contrast and tap targets are at least 44px, per the Figma plugin's checks.
